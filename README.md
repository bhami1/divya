# Her Own Little Universe ✦

A bespoke, cinematic personal website celebrating **Divya** — blending her MSc Physics identity, cherished photographs, radiant personality, and subtle cosmic aesthetics into an immersive interactive experience.

---

## 🌟 Features Included

1. **Dramatic Opening & Landing Screen**:
   - Poetic prologue: *"Some people are ordinary. Some people have their own universe."*
   - Glowing typography reveal of **DIVYA**.
   - Celestial pulse "Enter her universe" button.
   - Cinematic hyperdrive warp speed transition with dynamic canvas stardust streaks.

2. **The Centre of Her Universe**:
   - Centerpiece photo (`public/img.jpeg`) framed with rotating orbital rings and orbiting satellite planet.
   - Playful manifesto:
     - *MSc Physics student.*
     - *Professional overthinker.*
     - *Collector of memories.*
     - *And somehow, still one of my favourite humans.*
   - Celestial telemetry stat cards (Wavelength, Gravitational Pull, Favourite State, Constellation).

3. **Interactive Photo Constellation**:
   - Arranged like stars forming celestial constellations with dynamic SVG connector lines.
   - Irregular, artistic cosmic positioning featuring all supplied photos from `public/`.
   - Hover magnetic lift, glowing corona aura, and starlight chimes.
   - Fullscreen Lightbox with captions, date tags, and arrow/keyboard navigation.

4. **Physics × Her**:
   - Playful physics cards connecting her MSc Physics journey with her personality:
     - **Quantum State**: Existing in multiple states (studying, procrastinating, surviving).
     - **Relativity**: Time moves differently near assignment deadlines.
     - **Entropy**: Her room after one productive study session.
     - **Superposition**: "I'm tired" + scrolling for another hour.
     - **Gravity**: Attracting chaos wherever she goes.
     - **Photon Resonance**: Radiating vibrant energy lighting up every room.

5. **Celestial Wishes (Birthday & Friendship)**:
   - Golden foil parchment card with heartfelt wishes.
   - Interactive **"Release a Shooting Star"** button triggering glowing meteors across the sky.

6. **Cosmic Audio Atmosphere**:
   - Synthesized ethereal cosmic soundscape & starlight chimes powered by the Web Audio API.
   - 100% offline, zero external audio MP3 dependencies.

## 🚀 How to Run

### Method 1: Instant Direct Open
Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer to open it directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox!

### Method 2: Local HTTP Server (Recommended)
Run with Node.js in PowerShell or Terminal:
```bash
npx serve .
```
Then open `http://localhost:3000` (or the port shown).

---

## ✏️ How to Easily Edit Content & Texts

All customizable texts, quotes, photo titles, captions, and physics cards are centralized in:
📂 **`js/config.js`**

Open `js/config.js` in any text editor to modify:
- Landing texts and prologue
- Manifesto lines and titles
- Physics card titles, quotes, and formulas
- Photo captions and tags
- Birthday letter message and signature
